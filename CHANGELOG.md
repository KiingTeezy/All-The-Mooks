# Changelog

## 3.2.0 - Automatic updates - 2026-09-18

301 mods. Needs the server on 3.2.0. **Last re-import:** install from `All-The-Better-Mooks.zip` and the pack updates itself on every launch (packwiz).

- **Auto-update:** the new instance runs packwiz-installer before each launch and pulls the latest pack from GitHub. It comes with 10 GB of RAM and the server already in the Multiplayer list.
- **Map:** JourneyMap 6.0.8 replaces Xaero's Minimap, Xaero's World Map and XaeroPlus.
- Includes the 3.1.0 fixes (quest book logo, Bottled Letter, Biomes O' Plenty market/planter entries, quiet KubeJS warnings).

## 3.1.0 - The Exploration update - 2026-09-18

303 mods. Needs the server on 3.1.0.

- **New quests:** an Exploration group - Biomes O' Plenty (29 biomes), Structures (27), Creatures (20 to spot, 6 to defeat), plus the Archaeology and Fishing chapters from Better MC 6. 107 quests, rewarded from the pack's own loot tiers.
- **New mods:** Better Archaeology 1.3.8, Starcatcher 3.1.4.1.
- **Our own identity:** All The Better Mooks emblem on the title screen, window title, loading screen and quest book title; Discord status shows "Playing All The Better Mooks" with our logo; the title-screen and crash-screen help point to our Discord and bug-report form (the Reddit and server-host buttons are gone).
- **Fixes (same-day refresh):** Starcatcher's Bottled Letter is craftable, Biomes O' Plenty saplings and flowers are back in the Farming for Blockheads market and Agritech planters, the KubeJS warning screen is quiet, and the quest book's Welcome logo is ours.
- **Renamed (display names only - IDs, recipes and quests unchanged):** ATM Star -> Mook Star (plus Shard, Block and Fragments), Allthemodium -> Mookium (118 names across 8 mods, plus the quest text), All The Mods Trophy -> Better Mooks Trophy.

## 3.0.0 - All The Better Mooks - 2026-09-17

- **Newer than Better MC 6 ships, because they fix real bugs:** Biomes O' Plenty 26.1.2.0.22 (crash fix), Moog's Structure Lib 3.3.1 (server-freeze fix), Formations Overworld 1.0.5c (loot fix).

- **Kept:** all our server favourites and settings, including Better Days' long days.
- **Needs 10 GB of RAM** in Prism.
